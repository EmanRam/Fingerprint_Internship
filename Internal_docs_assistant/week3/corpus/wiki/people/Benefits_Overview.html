Benefits Overview

A quick summary of benefits. The policy portal is authoritative.

  • PTO: 20 days per year, up to 5 days carry over.
  • Sick leave: 10 days per year.
  • Learning budget: $1,500 per year.
  • Home-office stipend: $500 per year.
  • Parental leave: see the parental leave policy.
