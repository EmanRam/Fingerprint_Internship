IT tips for the sales team

  • Passwords need at least 12 characters and must be changed every 90 days - set a calendar reminder!
  • Use GlobalConnect when on customer Wi-Fi.
  • Demo laptops are shared: log out after every demo.
  • Lost a laptop at a trade show? Email IT the next morning.
