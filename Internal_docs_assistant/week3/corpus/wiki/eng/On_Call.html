On-call rotation

Fleet software engineers join the on-call rotation after 3 months.

  • Primary on-call stipend: $400 per week. Secondary: $200 per week.
  • No one is on call more than 1 week in 4.
  • Pages must be acknowledged within 15 minutes.
  • If you are paged between 00:00 and 06:00 you may start the next day up to 4 hours late.
