VPN Setup Guide

To work from home you need the GlobalConnect VPN.

  1. Install GlobalConnect from the Self-Service app catalog.
  2. Open it and enter the portal address vpn.northwind.example.
  3. Sign in with SSO and approve the MFA push.

The VPN is required when accessing internal systems from outside the office network.
