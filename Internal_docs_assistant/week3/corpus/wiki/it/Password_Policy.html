Password Policy

This page mirrors IT-STD-003 v2.0 for convenience. The policy portal copy is authoritative.

Corporate passwords must be at least 16 characters long. Passphrases are encouraged. Passwords are no longer rotated on a schedule; they must be changed immediately if compromise is suspected.

MFA is mandatory for all corporate accounts. Hardware security keys are mandatory for administrators and for anyone with production access. SMS codes are not an accepted second factor.

After 5 failed sign-in attempts the account locks for 30 minutes.
