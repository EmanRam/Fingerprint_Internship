All-hands Q&A (People Ops section)
Date: 2026-03-05

Q: I had 5 days left over from 2025. Do I lose 2 of them under the new PTO policy?
A: No. Days carried from 2025 are honoured in full, but they must be used by March 31, 2026.

Q: Does tenure count from my start date?
A: Completed years of service on January 1 are what count.
