# Northwind Robotics — IT Help Desk FAQ

## Accounts & Access
**Q: How do I reset my corporate password?**
Use the self-service portal at https://reset.northwind.example. Passwords must be
at least 14 characters and are rotated every 180 days. After 5 failed attempts
your account locks for 30 minutes.

**Q: How do I request access to a new system?**
File an access request in the IT portal under "Access → New Request." Approvals
route to your manager and the system owner. Standard turnaround is 2 business days.

## Hardware
**Q: What is the laptop refresh cycle?**
Laptops are refreshed every 3 years. Engineers may request a spec upgrade after
18 months with manager approval.

**Q: My laptop was lost or stolen — what do I do?**
Immediately report it to the Security team at security@northwind.example and file
a ticket marked URGENT. Security will remotely wipe the device. Do not wait until
the next business day.

## VPN & Security
**Q: How do I connect to the VPN?**
Install the GlobalConnect client from the Self-Service app catalog. Authenticate
with your corporate SSO and approve the MFA push. The VPN is required when
accessing internal systems from outside the office network.

**Q: What is the policy on using personal devices (BYOD)?**
Personal devices may access email and calendar only, and must have device
encryption and a screen lock enabled. Access to source code or customer data from
personal devices is prohibited.

## Software
**Q: How do I get a software license (e.g., JetBrains, Figma)?**
Request it through the IT portal's Software Catalog. Licenses under $50/month are
auto-approved; above that requires manager sign-off.
