# Northwind Robotics — HR Policies

## Expense Reimbursement
Business expenses must be submitted within 30 days via the Finance portal with
itemized receipts. Meals during travel are reimbursed up to $75/day domestic and
$110/day international. Alcohol is not reimbursable. Reimbursements are paid in
the next payroll cycle after approval.

## Travel Policy
Economy class is standard for flights under 6 hours; premium economy is permitted
for flights over 6 hours. Hotel bookings should not exceed $250/night without VP
approval. Always book through the corporate travel tool to get negotiated rates.

## Performance Reviews
Formal performance reviews happen twice per year, in June and December.
Compensation adjustments and promotions are decided in the December cycle.
Reviews use a 5-point scale and include peer feedback collected two weeks before.

## Referral Bonus
Employees who refer a candidate that is hired receive a $3,000 bonus, paid after
the new hire completes 90 days. There is no limit on the number of referrals.

## Training & Development
Each employee has a $1,500 annual learning budget for courses, books, and
conferences. Requests are approved by the direct manager. Unused budget does not
carry over to the next year.
