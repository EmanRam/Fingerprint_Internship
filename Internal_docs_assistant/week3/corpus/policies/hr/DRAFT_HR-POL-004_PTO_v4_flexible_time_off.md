# DRAFT — Flexible Time Off (proposed HR-POL-004 v4.0)

> **Status: DRAFT for comment. NOT APPROVED. Do not apply.**
> Comments to people-ops@northwind.example by 2026-10-31.

## Proposal

Replace accrued PTO with **Flexible Time Off (FTO)**: no fixed annual entitlement.
Employees take the time they need with manager approval, subject to a **minimum of
15 days per year** that every employee is expected to take.

## Carry-over

Under FTO there is no balance, so there is nothing to carry over and no payout on exit
(except where local law requires it).

## Open questions

- How does FTO interact with the Cairo office's statutory leave entitlement under
  Egyptian labour law?
- Should the minimum be 15 or 20 days?

## Proposed effective date

2027-01-01, subject to leadership approval.
