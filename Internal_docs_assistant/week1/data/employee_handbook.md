# Northwind Robotics — Employee Handbook (Excerpt)

## 1. Working Hours & Flexibility
Northwind Robotics operates on a flexible schedule. Core collaboration hours are
10:00–16:00 local time, during which employees are expected to be reachable.
Standard workweek is 40 hours. Employees may start any time between 07:00 and
10:00. Fully remote employees must overlap at least 4 hours with their team's
core hours.

## 2. Paid Time Off (PTO)
Full-time employees accrue 1.67 days of PTO per month (20 days per year). PTO
begins accruing on the first day of employment but cannot be used during the
first 90 days. Unused PTO up to 5 days may be carried into the next calendar
year; anything above 5 days is forfeited on December 31. PTO requests must be
submitted at least 5 business days in advance through the HR portal.

## 3. Sick Leave
Employees receive 10 paid sick days per year, separate from PTO. Sick leave does
not carry over. A doctor's note is required for absences longer than 3
consecutive days.

## 4. Remote Work Policy
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week by default. Fully remote
arrangements require director approval and a signed remote-work agreement.
Home-office stipend is $500 per year, reimbursed through the Finance portal.

## 5. Code of Conduct
Northwind Robotics is committed to a respectful, harassment-free workplace.
Violations should be reported to People Operations or via the anonymous ethics
hotline. Retaliation against anyone who reports in good faith is strictly
prohibited.

## 6. Parental Leave
Birth parents receive 16 weeks of fully paid leave. Non-birth and adoptive
parents receive 10 weeks of fully paid leave. Leave must be taken within 12
months of the birth or placement.
